/decl/material/liquid/accumulatedThis material type has mob effects based on accumulated dose. If the drug has been in your system enough to accumulate a dose, and the level of it in your bloodstream drops below a certain volume, you will get discontinuation effects. If it is above that volume or hasn't been in your system long enough to accumulate, you will get the positive effects.
